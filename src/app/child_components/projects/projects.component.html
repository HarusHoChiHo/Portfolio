A blog website

Description

In my third semester at Centennial College, I collaborated with another teammate to develop this website. The initial idea of this system was to streamline a restaurant's ingredients management. By setting the ingredient consumption for each dish, each time the staff make orders for customers, the system would reduce the amount of ingredients and reflect the changes on the admin portal. Restaurant managers are able to closely monitor the ingredient consumption and its stocks. That allows the managers make a clear plan on ingredients purchasing or promotion to accelerate some ingredients' consumption. Overall, it could simplify the ingredients management and prevent potential loss due to procuring surplus ingredients.
